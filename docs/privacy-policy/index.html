FitFlow Privacy Policy

Comprehensive data governance, AI telemetry safeguards, social feature protections, and regulatory compliance standards.

Application: FitFlow (v1.0.0 Build 1) Package: com.fitflow.fitflow Effective Date: September 2026 Module: IT3060 HCI Lab 06

1Introduction & Privacy by Design

FitFlow ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting user personal privacy and maintaining strict confidentiality over personal health, athletic performance, and nutritional records. FitFlow adheres to a Privacy-by-Design architecture, ensuring data minimization, end-to-end transport encryption, and explicit user consent mechanisms across all features.

Core Commitment: FitFlow never sells, rents, or monetizes user health records or biometric profiles with commercial data brokers or advertisers. All algorithmic personalization is executed with privacy-preserving methodologies.

2Data We Collect & Usage Purposes

We process only data strictly required to deliver personalized workout routines, nutritional analysis, and community interactions:

Data Category Specific Data Points Primary Purpose
Account Identity Full name, email address, profile picture Authentication, cloud synchronization, TestFlight feedback
Fitness Vitals Height, weight, heart rate peak, calories, workout logs AI progression recommendations and personal analytics

3AI Model Processing & Ephemeral Vision Analysis

FitFlow incorporates artificial intelligence models for dynamic training splits and real-time computer vision food identification:

  • Ephemeral Food Vision: Food photographs captured via the AI Meal Scanner are analyzed in ephemeral runtime memory to detect macronutrients. Raw image buffers are not permanently stored on external inference servers.
  • Algorithmic Anonymization: Where workout progression telemetry is utilized to improve internal coaching algorithms, records are stripped of all Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and aggregated.
  • Opt-In Model Analytics: Users can disable aggregated telemetry analytics at any time through the in-app Privacy & Settings modal.

4Social Features & Community Feed Controls

FitFlow provides opt-in community challenges and peer social streams:

  • Opt-In Public Visibility: Participation in global challenges (e.g., 30-Day Summer Shred Challenge) and posting workout milestones to the Community Activity Feed is strictly voluntary.
  • Granular Privacy Toggles: Users can switch their profile between Public, Friends-Only, and Stealth (Private) modes to conceal leaderboard positions and personal workout stats.
  • Content Moderation: Community streams are actively monitored, and users can report abusive content or block members immediately.

5Regulatory Compliance: GDPR & HIPAA Considerations

FitFlow aligns its health data processing procedures with international data protection and privacy frameworks:

🛡️ GDPR Compliance (EU / UK)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, users have the right to access, rectify, port, and permanently delete their data (Right to be Forgotten). Consent is explicitly requested before collecting health-related telemetry, and data processing agreements are maintained with cloud providers.

🏥 HIPAA Considerations

FitFlow is designed for personal wellness and fitness tracking rather than clinical medical diagnosis. However, health and vital records are handled under HIPAA-inspired administrative, physical, and technical security safeguards, including AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

6User Rights & Permanent Deletion

Users maintain full sovereignty over their records:

  • Export Data: Request a complete JSON/CSV export of workout history, macro logs, and profile records.
  • Account Deletion: Permanent deletion of account credentials, cloud logs, and active session tokens within 30 days of request.
  • Revoke Permissions: Camera, notification, and health permissions can be revoked anytime via system settings.
